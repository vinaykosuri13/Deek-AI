- Think before answering
- Search the internet when needed
- Remember important information
- Use tools

# Intent System

Deek understands user requests by identifying their intent.

## Supported Intents

### CHAT
General conversation and questions.

Example:
- What is Python?
- Explain TCP/IP

---

### SEARCH
Questions requiring current information.

Example:
- Latest AI news
- Weather today
- Bitcoin price

---

### CALCULATOR
Mathematical calculations.

Example:
- 45 × 87
- 100 / 25

---

### PHONE
Phone-related actions.

Example:
- Call Dad
- Dial Rahul

---

### MESSAGE
Messaging actions.

Example:
- Send "I'm coming" to Rahul

---

### APP
Open installed applications.

Example:
- Open YouTube
- Launch WhatsApp

---

### MEMORY
Remember or recall information.

Example:
- Remember my name is Vinay
- What is my name?
