# 🚀 DEEK AI ROADMAP

## Vision

**Deek (AIOS - Artificial Intelligence Operating System)** is Vinay's Personal AI Operating System.

Its mission is to understand, remember, reason, plan, execute, automate, and manage every aspect of Vinay's digital life.

Deek is not just a chatbot.

Deek is not just an AI assistant.

Deek is the intelligent operating layer that manages Vinay's digital world.

---

# Core Principles

Every feature added to Deek must satisfy at least one of these principles:

- Understand
- Remember
- Reason
- Plan
- Execute
- Automate
- Manage
- Improve

If a feature does not move Deek closer to its vision, it will not be built.

# Development Rules

Before writing code:

1. Define the feature.
2. Define the purpose.
3. Design the complete feature.
4. Implement completely.
5. Test completely.
6. Update GitHub.
7. Commit.
8. Update documentation.

No incomplete features.

No unnecessary file modifications.

One feature = One complete implementation.

---

# Long-Term Goal

Build Deek into Vinay's Personal AI Operating System that continuously grows in intelligence, capabilities, and autonomy while remaining modular, maintainable, and fully owned.
